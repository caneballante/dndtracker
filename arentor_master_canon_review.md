## Duplicate / Conflict Report

- **Blanders naming:** Merged `Blanders School of Adventuring`, `Blanders Everything Institute of Dungeoneering`, and `Blanders` as one organization because the source clearly treats them as the same institution. Canonical label still merits confirmation.
- **Brazen Banner naming:** Merged `Mercenaries’ Hall of the Brazen Banner` and the shortened `Brazen Banner`; the shorter form is clearly used for the same guild/company.
